Delivery Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Where we deliver

We serve MK Gold Coast, Yendada and nearby areas in Yendada, Visakhapatnam. We do not ship by courier or to other cities.

Pickup or delivery

  • When you order, choose I'll pick up or Please deliver.
  • There is no delivery charge.
  • If you chose pickup but can't come, let us know and we will deliver it to you.

When

  • Orders are usually packed the same day. Orders placed after 9 PM are packed the next morning.
  • We send you a WhatsApp message when your order is packed and ready, and you can follow its status under My orders.
  • Country chicken is handed over or delivered on Sunday.
